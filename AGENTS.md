# Chen Quant Center (CQC)

## Project Overview

Chen Quant Center is a quantitative research and market analytics platform.

The platform focuses on:

* Investment research
* Macroeconomic analysis
* Market data visualization
* Quantitative factor analysis
* Community forecasting

## Main Sections

### Research

Contains research articles and investment commentary.

Route:

/research

### Market

Contains stock market dashboards.

Route:

/market

Features:

* Price charts
* Historical data
* Technical indicators

### Quant Lab

Contains quantitative finance tools.

Route:

/quantlab

Features:

* SMA
* RSI
* Portfolio analytics
* Factor analysis

### Community

Contains market sentiment and forecasting features.

Route:

/community

Authentication is NOT required in Version 1.
